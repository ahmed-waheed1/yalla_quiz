# Food questions — review

`assets/questions/food.json`: 50 questions. Dishes, ingredients, cooking and cuisines. Questions about Arab dishes ask what they're made of or when they're eaten, never which country they come from; origin questions only use dishes with an undisputed home (hummus, falafel, baklava, kunafa, shawarma, tabbouleh, mansaf, kimchi, fondue and the like are left out). No alcohol.
Difficulty and region are for review only; they aren't stored in the asset.

- **Mix:** 20 Egyptian & Arab cuisine, 30 international.
- **Difficulty:** 20 easy, 20 medium, 10 hard.
- **Correct answer position:** 0 → 13, 1 → 13, 2 → 12, 3 → 12.
- **Confidence:** HIGH = sure of the fact and the wording in both languages. LOW = something to
  check (the note says what).
